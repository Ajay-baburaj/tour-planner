A morning in Thekkady starts with a decision about time. Forest department departures have check-in instructions and different meeting points from plantation tours. Confirm both before leaving your stay.

## On Periyar Lake

Book from the recognised operator and leave time for the transfer to the boarding point. Bring binoculars if you have them. The shoreline is worth watching for birds even when larger animals stay in the forest.

## A guided plantation walk

Ask a guide to show you pepper vines and cardamom at different stages. A walk through cultivated land can fill a warm afternoon at a slower pace than an extra drive.

## Plan the onward journey

Add extra time for bends on the high-range road, particularly after rain. If you are boarding a boat the same day, compare the two check-in times before booking either slot.
