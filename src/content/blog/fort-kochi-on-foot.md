Begin at the waterfront when the air is still cool. The counterweights of the Chinese fishing nets tell you a lot about how crews work: spend a few minutes watching a full lift and lower if the tide suits.

## From the water to town

Turn into the lanes behind Princess Street for shaded corners, cafés and old houses with deep verandas. Short detours beat following a single busy road. Cross to Mattancherry by tuk-tuk if the day is already hot or your group has young children.

## Allow time for Mattancherry

The palace deserves a slow visit. Its paintings are easiest to appreciate when you can pause. The Paradesi Synagogue has specific visitor hours, so check its local schedule before making it your only afternoon stop.

## Stay for an evening performance

When you book Kathakali, ask whether the venue allows visitors to see the make-up preparation. It offers context for the expressions and hand gestures that follow.
