The climb from Kozhikode deserves a day with some slack. Visibility can change across the bends, and lunchtime traffic often makes the last few kilometres slower than expected.

## Choose a base near one priority

A stay near Vythiri makes for an easy arrival day. If Edakkal is the main reason to go, choose a base that leaves room for an early drive and climb. Ask about opening days, the uphill approach and what access is like after wet weather.

## Spend an afternoon on an estate

Find a guided walk where the host can talk about shade trees and coffee processing. Ask whether the visit suits the age and mobility of everyone travelling. A one-hour walk can be plenty.

## Make the north coast a separate stop

Save time for Kozhikode on the way to Bekal. One night in town lets you try a market lunch and a waterfront walk. Then keep the first Bekal evening free for the fort or the coast.
