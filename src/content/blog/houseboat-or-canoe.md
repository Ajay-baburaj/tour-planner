The best boat depends on where you want to go. A broad, roofed kettuvallam has space for meals and beds. A small canoe fits into village lanes where a houseboat can’t turn.

## A houseboat for time on deck

Pick a day cruiser for a shaded lunch and an afternoon on the water. Reserve an overnight boat if you want to see the light change at the mooring and wake up on the bank. Confirm where the boat moors and when it stops moving for the evening.

## A canoe for narrow canals

An early canoe can pass under low crossings and reach paddy margins. Take a small bag, sun protection and water. Ask the guide how easy the jetty is to board and how exposed the route is to rain.

## Compare the full quote

Confirm the jetty, exact hours, meals, cabin AC and allowances for weather delays. If boarding with children or someone who needs assistance, ask for a photo of the jetty and talk through life jacket sizes and deck access.
