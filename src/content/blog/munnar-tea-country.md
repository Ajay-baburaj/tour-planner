You can cross Munnar town in a few minutes without traffic. Between Pallivasal and the estates beyond town, the pace is set by hairpins, school buses and weather. Choose one or two stops for the morning and keep a spare hour for the road.

## Leave the main road early

Start at your estate stay or arrange a plantation walk through your host. Ask where the walk is permitted before leaving the public road. Tea is a working crop, and several estates restrict entry on harvest days.

Carry light layers. Mist can arrive while you are walking, even when the lower road is clear. A flask of tea and a stop on a wide public viewpoint are often enough.

## What to pair with a walk

The Tea Museum explains the leaves you’ve just seen on the hillside. Mattupetty is a better follow-on if the group wants a short lakeside stop. Check park access before planning the Eravikulam shuttle around checkout.

## Give the drive its own day

If you are heading to a houseboat, confirm the boarding hour before choosing your Munnar departure. An early start leaves room for traffic and a proper meal. For a slower circuit, put one night in the backwaters after the hill drive and spend the following morning in a canoe.
