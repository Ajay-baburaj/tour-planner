Choose a room based on how you want to spend the evenings. North Cliff keeps cafés and views within easy reach. Odayam gives you a quieter starting point for an early walk.

## Walk north in the morning

Look for open public paths towards Odayam and step aside on narrow stretches. During rain, the laterite paths can get slippery. Turn back wherever the path edges are closed or eroded.

## Make one plan for the middle of the day

Find a shaded lunch or ask your accommodation about well-being sessions with a qualified local practitioner. Keep the rest of the afternoon free to follow the weather.

## Check the surf on the day

Sea conditions vary. Follow lifeguard directions and posted flags, and ask locally about safe access if travelling with children. For a calmer evening, watch the coast from the cliff path and leave the beach for morning.
